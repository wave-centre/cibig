The CIBiG team is pleased to welcome applicants for its **2026 cohort**.

### Programme overview

| Phase                                                   | Dates                              | Workload | Notes                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|  **On-line introductory course**                   | **5 Oct. – 16 Oct.**            | \~36h   | One week of synchronous & asynchronous sessions to prepare for the facetoface block |
|  **On-site intensive modules**                        | **26 Oct. – 20 Nov.** | \~153h  | Handson training at WAVE                                                             |
|  **Tutored internship on a bioinformatics platform** | **25 Jan.– 31 Mar.**        | 2 months | Conducted in your host institution under joint supervision                            |
|  **Internship restitution** | **March 25-27 2026**        | 13, 14, 15 Apr|                             |

The curriculum covers biology, NGS sequencing, Linux, Python/R, scientific reproducibility (conda, Singularity, Git, Snakemake), data analysis and visualisation.
