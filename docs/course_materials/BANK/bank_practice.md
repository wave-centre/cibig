# Lab Session: Advanced Search in NCBI and EMBL-EBI Databases

## Learning Objectives

At the end of this lab session, the student should be able to:

- perform advanced searches in [NCBI](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) and [EMBL-EBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/);
- extract nucleotide and protein sequences;
- identify and interpret genetic variants;
- search for raw sequencing data (SRA);
- analyze and organize retrieved biological information.
